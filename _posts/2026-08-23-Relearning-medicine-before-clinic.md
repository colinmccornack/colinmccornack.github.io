My time in the PhD part of my MD/PhD is nearing its end. Every year my program holds a meeting as part of the weekly seminar series where current students discuss returning to clinic and applying to residency. At the most recent iteration of this, a contentious recommendation of studying before returning was made. As I'm currently waiting on the reviews for my paper, much of my time has been spent writing and setting up for potential experiments that may be requested by reviewers. Alongside this, I wanted to brush up on my knowledge before returning so that the firehose is a little less full when I return to clinic. 

Before I started the PhD, I was an Anki devotee. It was the main tool I used to study for my preclinical exams and for Step 1 (way back when it had a score...). During my time in graduate school, I've seen the studying process for Step 1/Step 2 become increasingly optimized, and the scores between specialties become flatter and flatter (as discussed in the [Sheriff of Sodium’s video on this year’s version of his Charting Outcomes reflection](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r3O2fwq5YO0)). Step 1 no longer is used as the separator, and the median/mean Step 2 score has increased by ~5-10 points since I started medical school, and this was enough of a motivating factor to make the effort to study before returning to put my best foot forward in the coming years of clinical work and learning. 

Balancing studying the material that I am supposed to know felt like an insurmountable hurdle, but I’ve tried to make it easier for myself by breaking it down into smaller and more manageable pieces. To do this, I took the most recent iteration of the Anking Step Deck and extracted the number of cards that correspond to individual tags within 3rd party resources, so I could see how many cards fell into each subject field, and load this into Notion to create a tracker to use to measure my progress. Specifically, I wanted to tackle the cards that contain the `#AK_Other::Only_Step_1&2_Overlap`, which is used for cards with information that is tested on both exams, i.e. information I am expected to remember from taking Step 1. I exported individual files containing the card information for the Step 1/Step 2 tag and third party resources, specifically [Boards and Beyond](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1eSa-dYsIdK27rPzBoAWl3wc83KNV6e-l/view?usp=drive_link) and [Bootcamp](https://drive.google.com/file/d/175S0bTG0LlaUTQKPpkCAvgO-brcwsbNd/view?usp=sharing), both of which are linked. You can generate these yourself by using the [script found here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1AtFV6vyKGLfOGzx5-CA87jprWbte_rtf/view?usp=sharing). You can make a little tracker in Notion using Rollups and Relations if you want to track the number of lectures/lessons you’ve watched. Bootcamp didn’t exist when I was studying for Step 1 and I wanted to see how it overlaps with Boards and Beyond for content, so I had an LLM generate a static visualizer of these overlaps [which can be found here](https://colinmccornack.github.io/bootcamp-bnb-overlap/). I thought it would be more interesting or useful, but I can’t say it’s very surprising to see that the cards tagged as Nephrotic Syndrome from B&B overlap well with those from Bootcamp. More useful than this might be pages with the number of cards per resource ([Boards & Beyond](https://colinmccornack.github.io/bnb-lessons/) and [Bootcamp](https://colinmccornack.github.io/bootcamp-lessons/)) that overlap with the `#AK_Other::Only_Step_1&2_Overlap` tag, which can be found above.
